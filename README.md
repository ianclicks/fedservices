# Violet storefront

A responsive purple-themed static storefront starter.

## Files
- `index.html` — home page and 4 product cards
- `products/` — individual product pages
- `checkout.html` — PayPal / crypto checkout UI demo
- `style.css` — all styling
- `script.js` — product prefill and demo checkout interaction
- `images/` — put your own product images here

## Add your images
Keep these exact filenames, or update the `src` attributes in the HTML:
- `images/nitro.jpg`
- `images/server-boosts.jpg`
- `images/aged-account.jpg`
- `images/discord-members.jpg`

## Publish with GitHub Pages
Upload the contents of this folder to your repository. In GitHub, open **Settings → Pages**, choose **Deploy from a branch**, select your branch and `/ (root)`, then save.

## Important
This is a front-end template. The checkout only demonstrates the UI and does not accept payments or store customer details. To take real payments, integrate PayPal and a reputable crypto payment processor through their official hosted checkout/server-side APIs. Never put secret keys in `script.js`. Confirm that every product and delivery method complies with Discord's current terms and applicable laws. In particular, do not offer fake engagement or account transfers where prohibited.
